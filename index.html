💡 How to Use NEXUS

  1. Click Open NEXUS Agent to launch the IBM Bob agent.
  2. Tell the agent your TLS area (e.g. "I'm from Sales Support Center").
  3. Ask your process-related question in plain language.
  4. NEXUS will search the correct OneDrive folder and return the answer with a direct document link.
  5. If no document is found, the agent will advise you to contact the process owner.

📋 Example Questions

  • What is the process for quote validation under Sales Support Center?
  • Where can I find the latest EMEA BP process guide?
  • What are the steps for ASEANZK process handling?
  • Show me the process document for EMEA International billing support.
  • Who should I contact if the process document is outdated?
